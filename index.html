Our Story

AURASCENT began with a simple observation: most people choose a perfume by what is popular, not by what feels like them. We wanted to make that choice calmer and more personal.

Our catalog is small on purpose. Each fragrance belongs to a clear scent family, such as floral, woody, fresh, oriental, citrus, or gourmand, so you can compare notes instead of scrolling through hundreds of bottles.

Whether you are wearing your first perfume or looking for the one you will be remembered by, we help you find a scent that is unmistakably yours.
